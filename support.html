FAQ / 常见问题

  • Is there an account? No. The App works offline without registration.
    需要账号吗？ 不需要。本应用完全单机，无注册登录。
  • Where are my photos stored? On your device only. We do not host a cloud album.
    照片存在哪里？ 只在您的设备上。我们不做云相册。
  • Why does the App ask for location? Only if you add place/GPS watermark components to burn text into the image.
    为什么要定位？ 仅当您添加地点/经纬水印组件并烧进画面时使用。
  • Why ads or tracking prompts? International builds may show rewarded ads via AdMob and request ATT. China storefront builds do not show ads. You can decline tracking.
    为什么有广告/跟踪提示？ 国外构建可能通过 AdMob 激励广告并请求 ATT；中国区不上广告。您可拒绝跟踪。
  • How do purchases work? China: per-module or all-tools unlock after free exports. International: rewarded ad for one export, or subscribe to unlock / remove ads. Restore via the App Store.
    如何购买？ 中国区：免费导出次数后可买断模块或总包。国外：看激励广告换单次导出，或订阅解锁/去广告。换机请用 App Store 恢复购买。
  • Manage subscription? Open App Store → Subscriptions.
    如何管理订阅？ 打开 App Store 订阅管理页面。
  • ID photo accepted by agencies? No claim. The tool is a layout aid only.
    证件照能直接办证吗？ 不宣称机关受理，仅辅助排版。
