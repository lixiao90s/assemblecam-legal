Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

How AssembleCam / 汇摄影 handles permissions and data / 了解本应用如何处理权限与信息

Last updated / 最后更新：September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AssembleCam ("汇摄影", "we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (Bundle ID: com.lixiao.app.cameratool).

本隐私政策说明 AssembleCam（汇摄影，「我们」或「本应用」）在您使用移动应用（Bundle ID：com.lixiao.app.cameratool）时如何收集、使用与共享信息。

1. Core Principles / 核心原则

  • Fully offline by design — Photos, videos, and drafts stay on your device. We do not operate a cloud album or send your media to our servers.
    完全单机 — 照片、视频与草稿只留在您的设备。我们不做云相册，也不把素材传到我们的服务器。
  • No account system — The App does not require registration or login.
    无账号体系 — 本应用不要求注册或登录。
  • No ads, no tracking — The App does not display ads and does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework or advertising identifiers (IDFA) for tracking.
    无广告、无追踪 — 本应用不展示广告，不使用 ATT / IDFA 进行用户追踪。
  • Purchases via the store — One-time (non-consumable) in-app purchases are processed by Apple / Google; we do not receive your payment card details. There are no subscriptions.
    购买由商店处理 — 一次性（非消耗型）内购由 Apple / Google 处理，我们不获取您的支付卡信息。无订阅。

2. Device Permissions / 设备权限

  • Camera — Capture photos and videos; dual capture; ID photo framing; optional selfie with fill light.
    相机 — 拍照录像、前后双录、证件照取景；补光时可边补光边自拍。
  • Microphone — Record audio with video when you choose to capture sound.
    麦克风 — 仅在您选择录制带声音的视频时使用。
  • Photo Library — Import media into tools and save / share exports to your library.
    相册 — 导入素材到工具，以及将结果保存/分享到系统相册。
  • Location — Used only when you add place / GPS watermark components, to burn text into the image on device. We do not continuously track you or upload location history.
    定位 — 仅在您添加地点/经纬水印组件时用于本机烧进画面。我们不会持续追踪或上传位置历史。

Fill-light lamp mode does not require the camera. / 补光灯「灯」模式不需要相机。

3. Information We May Process / 可能处理的信息

  • On-device media — Processed locally for capture, editing, and export; not uploaded to our servers.
    本机媒体 — 拍摄、编辑与导出均在本地完成，不上传到我们的服务器。
  • Device / app settings — Language preference, tool settings, free-export counters, and entitlement flags stored on device.
    设备与应用设置 — 语言、工具偏好、免费导出次数与权益标记保存在本机。
  • Purchase receipts — Verified via the platform store / StoreKit for one-time unlocks; no media content is attached.
    购买凭证 — 通过平台商店/StoreKit 校验一次性解锁，不附带媒体内容。

4. Third-Party Services / 第三方服务

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — Distribution, one-time purchases, and restore.
    分发、一次性购买与恢复购买。
  • Apple Privacy Policy

We do not integrate advertising SDKs (including Google AdMob) in the current App version.

当前版本不集成广告 SDK（包括 Google AdMob）。

5. How We Use Information / 信息用途

  • Provide capture, editing, and export features on your device / 在本机提供拍摄、编辑与导出功能
  • Process one-time purchases and restore entitlements via the store / 通过商店处理一次性购买与恢复权益
  • Respond to support emails you send us / 回复您主动发送的支持邮件

6. Data Sharing / 数据共享

We do not sell your personal information. Media stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself. Purchase-related data may be processed by Apple (or Google on other stores) only as needed to operate those services.

我们不会出售您的个人信息。媒体留在您的设备上，除非您自行导出或分享。购买相关数据可能由 Apple（或其他商店由 Google）在提供服务所必需的范围内处理。

7. Your Choices / 您的选择

  • Permissions: Revoke camera, mic, photos, or location in system Settings.
    可在系统设置中关闭相机、麦克风、相册或定位权限。
  • Purchases: Restore one-time unlocks via Settings → Restore Purchases in the App, or App Store account purchase history.
    购买：可在应用设置 → 恢复购买，或通过 App Store 账号购买记录恢复一次性解锁。

8. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

9. Changes / 变更

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised Policy.

我们可能更新本政策；顶部更新日期会随之变化。更新后继续使用即视为接受修订后的政策。
